# I28 SUMMARY — phosphosite turnover atlas (saved by PI from agent message)
Definition: family F × confident substrate (PSP + kinaseSub top kinase prob≥.3 & Cantley≥90); verified inhibition (I26 rule, leave-site-out); single-agent ≤120 min (or unknown time); R = mean robust z over study×cell experiments; fast (R≤−1, ≥50% exps z≤−1), stable (R>−.5, <25%). 2,605 units / 2,476 sites: 643 fast, 912 intermediate, 1,050 stable. Matched random controls. tables/turnover_atlas.tsv.
1. **Turnover is a reproducible site property**: fast fraction EGFR 50%, ERK 35%, MTOR 24%, AKT 21%, CDK 21% (random 2–9%); cross-study split-half ρ .34 (random .16; .30 after removing cross-family response); ERK .47, MTOR .49, EGFR .70, AKT .27, CDK .10. PSP+predictor edges fast 38% vs PSP-only 20%.
2. **Poorly predicted by sequence/structure**: LOSO (held-out sites) AUROC .545 (features) vs .725 (same site in other studies). Significant: Thr acceptor OR 1.27, low pLDDT/IDR faster, acidic ±5 OR .83, basic −3 OR .74 — consistent with phosphatase preferences (PP2A-B55/PP1 pThr; Cundell 2016; MRBLE:Dephos 2023). Null: functional score, PSP regulatory, age, docking motifs, motif %ile, abundance.
3. **Fast sites are tumour reporters**: CPTAC median ρ with family footprint .27 fast vs .13 stable vs .03 random (CI optimistic). **Library pruning to fast edges improves activity inference on 17 edited held-out studies**: +.032 [.009,.059] (p .02) vs random +.003; all 33 studies +.007–.010 ns.
Negatives: only 5 families populated (AKT/CDK 2 studies, mostly Hijazi); CK2/ATM/ATR/PKA/p38/PLK1 untestable; unknown-time conditions included; stable ≠ non-substrate.
Novelty: no human cross-kinase compilation found (Kanshin 2017 yeast; Cundell 2016; Kauko 2015). Self C3.5/N4/P3.5.
