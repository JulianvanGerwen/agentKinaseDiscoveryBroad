# I08 SUMMARY — abundance vs PTM control of kinase activity in tumours (saved by PI from agent message)
Setup: 10 CPTAC cohorts, 1,030 tumours; activity recomputed with all kinase-protein sites masked (no self/paralog sites); meanz (PSP) + core-v3 on protein-residualised substrates; rank partial r adjusted for purity, ESTIMATE, proliferation; random-effects meta + LOCO; nulls: 200 size/residue-matched random substrate sets, other kinases' protein.

1. **Activity ≠ abundance; activating site tracks activity.** Own-protein partial r median 0.02 (meanz, 88 kinases) / 0.016 (core-v3, 380); mRNA 0.02. Activating site beats protein in 37/54 (p=0.003) and 81/117 (p<1e-4). PSP-annotated activating sites beat unannotated (0.063 vs 0.031, p=0.008). PTM-controlled in both methods (100% LOCO, random-set z>3): MAPK1 Y187 r 0.45 [0.33,0.55], MAPK3 Y204 0.39, PTK2 Y576 0.61/0.41, MTOR S2448 0.26, PAK1 S204 0.27, ROCK1 S1341 0.30, PRKD1 S910 0.24, RPS6KA1 S380 0.29. Novelty partly known (Arshad 2019 MCP, 2 cohorts, uncorrelated abundance). Self C4/N3/P4.
2. Abundance-coupled: only CDK1 (0.20), CDK2 (0.17), RPS6KA5 (0.20) robust; purity+proliferation adjustment removes >half of raw couplings; family/proliferation caveat. Known. C3/N2/P2.
3. Genomics doesn't explain decoupled kinase activity: held-out ρ mutations 0.00, regulator CNV 0.00, regulator protein 0.07, other kinases' activity 0.78; 0/325 mutation, 0/1,105 CNV reproducible tests. Known cascades enriched among co-active kinases (OR 2.4). Useful negative. C3/N2/P2.
4. **Candidate new activity-reporter sites** (both methods, FDR<.05, LOCO≥80%, ≥4 cohorts, own kinase top-10%): unannotated in PSP regulatory: PAK2 S197 (r .41/.43; known autophos), IKBKB S675/S697 (known autophos cluster), **CDK13 T588** (.29/.45), **PTK2 S840** (.52/.33), **ABL1 Y185** (.35/.30). Peptides unique. C3/N4/P4.
Negatives: inhibitory-site signs method-dependent (GSK3B S9); RD vs non-RD weak; dark-kinase reporter sites only core-v3; ERK1/2 and CDK1/2 family-level; FAK Y576 co-varies with SRC.
