# I27 SUMMARY — allele-specific signalling outputs of oncogenic mutations (CPTAC, 1,030 tumours)
Pre-registered in `PREREG.md` before any outcome was computed. Readouts reused from b_I24 (stage composites), infra_cptac (family-level kinase activity, protein-adjusted sites) and b_I10 (perturbation footprints). Primary variant protein-adjusted (`tc_padj`); OLS adjusted for purity + E2F (+ subtype), HC3 95% CI, Freedman–Lane permutation p (5,000), MDE = 2.8·SE. Caveat: with these n the permutation p is often more conservative than the HC3 CI; the pre-declared permutation p decides every verdict.

## Exploratory (8,054 tests, 28 at FDR 10%)
1. **Driver alleles set ERK output in LUAD in the opposite direction to the cell-line expectation.** EGFR-mutant tumours have ERK activation-loop (TEY) output **−1.11 SD** (ex19del, p<.001) / **−0.86** (L858R, p .003) and ERK-substrate output −0.65 / −0.55 vs EGFR-WT LUAD, while receptor pY is *not* lower (+0.32 / +0.41, ns); KRAS G12C is **+0.84** (p .003). Robust to smoking adjustment and present in `tc`. The MAP2K3–7 "family" scores that first flagged this are one degenerate feature (pairwise r .97–.99) and were collapsed. This is allele-level RTK→ERK decoupling and matches I24 finding 2 and I10 finding 2 (EGFR-amp GBM).
2. **PDAC KRAS G12R has a MAPK *excess*, not an AKT deficit**: vs G12D, RAF-inhibitor footprint −1.07 [−1.53,−0.61] q .015 (i.e. less RAF-inhibited), ERK-substrates +0.76 p .004, RSK-family activity +0.80–1.00 (q .12–.15). The pre-registered PI3K deficit was in the right direction but not resolvable; the detectable allele difference is in the MAPK arm.
3. **PIK3CA mutants raise ERK output**: helical vs WT ERK +0.36 [0.11,0.62] p .006 (5 cohorts); BRCA kinase-domain vs WT ERK +0.74 q .056. Cross-talk, not allele-class discrimination.
4. KRAS G12V vs WT (3 cohorts): PRAS40-T246 −0.73 [−1.11,−0.36] p<.001, AKTSUB −0.53 p .003 — mutant KRAS with *lower* AKT-substrate output than KRAS-WT.

## Novelty / self-score
Allele-resolved human-tumour phosphoproteomic contrasts for these specific predictions have not to my knowledge been published; the mechanisms themselves (1–4 above) are all known in cell lines, so the contribution is the tumour-level test and its mostly negative result. Hit 1 is the most novel (allele-specific, direction-reversed vs cell lines, replicates two prior branch findings by a different route). **Compelling 3 / Novel 3 / Promising 3** overall; hit 1 alone C4/N3/P3.

## Negatives (powered or not)
P1, P4 and P5 give no significant stage-composite difference; P2 and P3 are underpowered by their own pre-declared MDE, and P3's helical-vs-kinase question cannot be answered in CPTAC at any cohort (max n 12 vs 6 per cohort). Cross-cohort exploratory meta is nearly empty: only 15 allele contrasts anywhere reach n≥8 per arm, and almost none recur across cohorts. No allele contrast survives FDR on the pre-specified AKT/SGK sites. The permutation p and HC3 CI disagree in sign-of-significance for several small-n BRAF/PIK3CA contrasts — those are reported as not significant.
