# val_I03 — discriminating tests for I03 nominations (direct vs downstream vs artefact)
**Data.** Compendium robust per-condition z (study×agent×cell units, doses ≥100 nM); decryptM curves (b_I17; A549 AZD8055/dactolisib/pictilisib/MK2206/MEKi); CPTAC protein-adjusted tumour residuals (10 cohorts, partial correlation, Stouffer); PSP; web. Scripts `scripts/val_I03/01–06`; tables `tables/` (`verdicts.tsv`, `class_effects.tsv`, `matched_ratios.tsv`, `dose_pEC50.tsv`, `cptac_meta.tsv`, `t3_mapk_concordance.tsv`, …); figures `figures/fig_T1_mtor.png`, `fig_T2_kpna4.png`, `fig_T3_epn1.png`.
**Control check.** The tests separate the controls. Direct mTORC1 sites (PRAS40 S183, 4EBP1 T46/S65, LARP1 S766) do not move under S6K inhibitors (S6Ki/mTORi ratio about 0) and respond only weakly to AKT inhibitors (AKTi/mTORi 0–0.3). Under pictilisib their pEC50 is 5.4–5.7, versus 6.75–7.0 for AKT sites. S6K sites (EIF4B S422, RPS6 S236/240) are rapamycin-sensitive (ratio 0.9–1.2) and fall under S6K inhibitors (EIF4B S422 ratio 0.18–0.37). Caveat: RPS6 S235 single-site peptides go up under mTOR inhibitors (a peptide-isoform artefact), so they are unusable. The PRKAA1 S356 positive control is noisy and also PI3K-responsive.

**Untestable in the compendium.** (c) Timing: there are no mTOR-inhibitor time series. The only one, MK2206 at 30 vs 60 min in A549, gives the same 30/60 ratio for LAMTOR1 S98 (1.3), PRAS40 S183 (1.9) and PRAS40 T246 (1.8). (e) Stimulation is sparse. RRAGC S15 rises with NGF (+3.8) together with LARP1 S766 (+4.4). LAMTOR1 S98 is flat under EGF/FGF (|z|≤1.3).
**Literature** (none of the five sites has a functional or kinase report):
- RRAGC S15: PSP 16 HTP / 0 LTP. The mTORC1 feedback sites are S21 and T394 (Yang et al., EMBO J 2018/19).
- LAMTOR1 S98: 12 HTP. No Ragulator-p18 site has been reported; the only one known is LAMTOR4 S67, a PKA site.
- TBC1D5 S730: 9 HTP. Nutrient/MTOR-regulated dephosphorylation of TBC1D5 has been reported (Carosi et al., Autophagy 2024), but the sites are not named and it is not needed for autophagic capture of retromer.
- KPNA4 S60: 1 LTP + 25 HTP. Hazawa et al. (Oncogene 2019) inferred "MAPK-dependent" from 24 h U0126 plus a pan-pSer pull-down: no site-specific readout, CK2 not tested. The acute data here point to CK2, and the long-term U0126 effect is likely indirect.
- EPN1 S435: 19 HTP. Not reported; only CDK1/mitotic epsin phosphorylation is known.
**Biology (hypotheses).** mTORC1 may phosphorylate its own docking machinery at the lysosome. That would extend the RagC S21/T394 feedback (RagC S15 is in the same N-terminal tail) to the Ragulator membrane anchor p18 (S97/98, in the linker between the lipidated N-terminus and the Rag-binding region). These could tune Rag–Ragulator assembly or lysosome positioning, or mTORC1 release, as the Yang destabilisation model would predict. TBC1D5 S730 sits in the retromer/RAB7-GAP region linking endosome maturation to autophagy, but it is better read as a growth-factor (RSK/S6K/AKT) input than as direct mTORC1. Next tests: in vitro mTORC1 kinase assay on RagC N-tail and p18 peptides; Raptor KO vs S6K1/2 DKO; S/A mutants and their effect on Rag–Ragulator binding and on TFEB/4EBP1 output. For KPNA4: CX-4945 and CK2α/α′ KO vs MEKi with a phospho-S60 readout. For EPN1: ERK2 in vitro assay; S435A effect on EGFR internalisation.
