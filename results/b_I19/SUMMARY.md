# I19 SUMMARY — feedback & compensation under kinase inhibition (saved by PI from agent's final message; agent write was blocked)

Data: 468 high/medium-confidence single-agent inhibitor conditions → 310 experiments (study × cell × drug), 43 studies, 31 drug classes; within study×cell centring; Klaeger/Hijazi binders masked.

1. **Up/down asymmetry (main).** Cross-study replication of DOWN sites rises with effect size (0.20→0.29→0.39 for |log2FC| 0.5–1/1–2/>2; null 0.12→0.16→0.22); UP sites flat (0.12→0.13→0.14; null 0.08–0.10). Invariant sites (≥3 studies) up vs down: MAPK 8 vs 130 (null 1.6/10.3), MEK 4 vs 138, mTOR 2 vs 18. Decomposition: same cell line / different drug same class retains replication (up +0.35, down +0.28); changing cell line or study destroys it (up +0.08/+0.01). Down-invariance predicted by PSP regulatory (OR 1.42), functional score; up-invariance only weakly (disorder, pTyr). Survives uncentred data and exclusion of massive-down experiments. Novelty: partly (Hill 2017 context-specific edges); asymmetry not reported. Self: C4/N4/P4.
2. **Self-hyperphosphorylation catalogue.** 329 tests: 17 up / 29 down (q<0.05). AKT1 S473/T308 under ATP-competitive AKTi (known, positive control). **PAK4 S148 +2.1 under PF-3758309, 3/3 cell lines, single study (Hijazi) — no prior report.** PRKCA T501 (known priming protection), MEK1 S222 (known), ERK2 T185 under ERKi (known from memory), CDK1 Y15 (known). Self: C3/N4/P3 for PAK4.
3. **Kinase-level atlas** (10,104 tests; 22 up, 62 down q<0.05). Controls recovered: RAF paradox (ERK2 +1.0 BRAF-WT vs −2.1 V600; type-II pan-RAF ≈0), AKTi→ERK up, rapalog→AKT up. **MET/ALK inhibitors → Hippo/NDR kinases up (STK38/STK38L/LATS1/2, z_core ≈+1.0, 6–7 studies, 79–81% experiments up, null p≤0.007, LOSO min +0.67; YAP1 S127 +0.66)** — caveat ~half signal from YAP1 sites. Partly known (EGF→PI3K–PDK1 represses LATS, Fan 2013; EGFR→MOB1, Ando 2021). Self C3/N3/P3. MEKi→ULK1 up (known autophagy link).

Negatives: most other "up" kinase hits driven by 1–3 predicted-substrate sites (artefacts); classic transcriptional feedback (MEKi→EGFR/AKT) invisible (data mostly ≤1 h); invariance predictors explain little; power limited (2–8 studies/class); up fraction correlates with down fraction per experiment (ρ 0.70).
