## 4. Claims at risk or overstated
- **Finding A as "key" and "decisive mirror test".** The audit's own verdict is "survives with qualification, C3 by its own estimate". The inhibitor arm leans on MEK (leave-MEK-out ΔLOR 0.20, CI includes 0) and is absent at ≤30 min; DDR inhibitors show the reverse; class specificity CIs include 0. Replication is conditioned on co-measured sites and a log-odds metric, which can reflect convergent-node selection (heavily measured, high-variance sites) rather than "layers". "Two layers" and "reverse = compensatory" are interpretation, not shown. I05 shows timing does not separate them, and I22/I23 are untested. The I19 summary's "up responses reproducible within cell" (r .62 vs .22) might equally reflect shared control and batch effects (the audit's null for different-class/same-cell is LOR 1.2-1.6, so about half of the within-cell signal is batch).
- **"~2 mM intracellular ATP" (I17.1).** It is derived from 5 drugs and 3 Km values, with a permutation p of 0.05 for the Km-aware model, and it assumes target-substrate sites reflect direct target occupancy. Nothing in the data constrains it away from 1.5-3.6 mM only because that is the prior range. Present it as a consistency check, not a measurement. The allosteric MEKi "overshoot" rests on a trametinib replication and on MK2206 being an exception.
- **I17.2** (motif-predicted substrates more potent) may reflect predictor bias toward high-stoichiometry or high-abundance sites rather than potency.
- **I08.3 reporter sites**: V1 retained only IKBKB S697 (an already-known autophosphorylation cluster) and rejected most of the rest. "Novel reporter" language in the I08 SUMMARY should be removed; the tree still lists it as a candidate.
- **Activity-inference claims at kinase level** (D1/I19): family-level non-identifiability and the autophosphorylation circularity found in V1 affect all stored scores. Any earlier branch that used stored scores (I19.3, I10/I06 if started before masking) should be re-run masked.
- **Tree status text** says "Negatives ... PAK4 S148 and most reporters (failed cross-validation)". That is stronger than the evidence: PAK4 and CDK13 were untestable, not refuted. Use "unreplicated".
